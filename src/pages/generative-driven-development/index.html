How GenDD works

AI at every stage. Experienced engineers in control.

GenDD combines AI agents, continuously managed context, repeatable workflows, and experienced practitioners to accelerate the entire development lifecycle.

01

Context that compounds

Requirements, architecture, business rules, and key decisions are maintained as shared context so engineers and agents work from the same foundation.

02

AI across the lifecycle

Agents support planning, architecture, development, testing, documentation, and delivery—not just code generation.

03

Humans remain accountable

Senior engineers direct the agents, make critical decisions, validate outputs, and own what gets delivered.

04

Quality built in

Testing, security, reliability, and human review are embedded throughout delivery—not left until the end.

Engagement models

Three ways to put GenDD to work

Embed GenDD practitioners in your team, establish the operating model across your organization, or bring in a compact team to own delivery.

Embed

Forward Deployed Engineers

Embed one or more senior GenDD practitioners directly inside your existing product or engineering team.

They:
  • Work within your current sprints and delivery workflows
  • Build production software alongside your team
  • Apply GenDD to active priorities from day one
  • Add senior AI-driven development expertise where you need it

Best when: You need experienced GenDD builders integrated into an existing team.

Establish

GenDD Scaffolding

Establish the structures, workflows, and guardrails required to make GenDD repeatable across your organization.

We help you:
  • Assess your current development environment and AI readiness
  • Establish shared context, workflows, standards, and team practices
  • Configure reusable agents, tools, quality gates, and golden paths
  • Put the operating model to work on real development initiatives

Best when: You want your internal teams to adopt and scale GenDD as a consistent way of working.

Own

Agentic AI Pod

Bring in a compact senior team that uses GenDD to own and deliver a defined product, platform, automation, or modernization initiative.

Your pod provides:
  • Cross-functional product, engineering, and quality expertise
  • End-to-end ownership from planning through production
  • Working software delivered in rapid increments
  • Quality, security, and governance throughout delivery

Best when: You need a complete team to own an outcome or the scope requires more than embedded individual engineers.
