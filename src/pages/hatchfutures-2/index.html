The Philosophy

Not a nonprofit.
A mindset.

HatchFutures is the answer to a question every person in tech should be asking: what am I doing to make sure this is not just for the few?

Technology moves fast. The gap between those who build it and those who never get the chance keeps growing. HatchFutures is how HatchWorks acts on that, starting from the communities where our team lives and works across North and South America.

No bureaucracy. Just people with skills passing them on to the people who need them most.

  • Technology should be democratic

    Access to technology education should not depend on where you were born or how much your school has in its budget.

  • The gap is closeable

    The skills gap is not a natural law. It is a systemic failure. And systemic failures can be fixed by people who decide to act.

  • From your field of action

    You do not need to quit your job to change things. HatchFutures is proof that companies can build real impact into how they operate, not as an afterthought.

  • Youth first, always

    We focus on young people because that is where the seed grows furthest. Skills acquired early become careers, communities, and cycles broken.

Why Educators

One teacher reaches
hundreds of students.

We train the people who plant the seed. Educators who understand technology can change classrooms, careers, and communities at scale. That is where our focus goes, and that is why we built two pathways for them to start.

How We Show Up

Three ways
we act on it.

Every program is designed to create a real path — not just a moment.

  • 01

    AI Workshops

    One-day sessions run by HatchWorks team members in English and Spanish. We go into schools, bring real tools, and show young people what a career in tech actually looks like.

  • 02

    Real Internships

    Up to 6 months working alongside HatchWorks engineers and business professionals on actual client projects. The best interns get a job offer, here or at a partner company.

  • 03

    Volunteering and Giving

    Every Hatcher dedicates three days per year to community work. Clothing drives, fundraising, and partnerships with Girls Who Code, Year Up, and Bright Futures Atlanta.
