Top 10 AI Podcasts You Shouldn’t Miss in 2026

  • Matt Paige
  • March 20, 2025
  • Updated: December 23, 2025

If you’re as passionate about AI as I am—and trust me, that means you’re always on the lookout for fresh perspectives—then you’re in the right place.

I’m excited to share my pick of the top AI podcasts that are not only keeping pace with rapid technological innovation but also breaking down complex topics into practical, everyday insights.

The Top AI Podcasts

In 2026, artificial intelligence is evolving faster than ever, transforming industries, reshaping work, and even changing how we think about our day-to-day lives.

Whether you’re an engineer, a business leader, or just curious about where AI is headed, these top 10 AI podcasts are your ticket to staying ahead of the curve.

#1. Talking AI

Top of our list is none other than Talking AI—hosted by yours truly.

In each episode, I sit down with AI experts, industry leaders, innovators, and early adopters to discuss everything from technical breakthroughs to the ethical debates that shape our world.

What sets Talking AI apart is its balanced approach. We delve deep into complex topics such as reasoning models, agents, and prompt engineering while also exploring how you can tactically apply these concepts in your business. All the while, our conversations remain engaging and relatable through personal anecdotes, humor, and reflections on everyday challenges.

Whether you’re looking for the latest trends, a nuanced discussion on the future of work, or just an engaging story about how AI is transforming lives, Talking AI has you covered.

It’s not just about technology—it’s about understanding how AI can empower us, improve our businesses, and even change the way we live. So if you want to be part of the conversation that’s shaping the future of artificial intelligence, join us on Talking AI.

#2. Dwarkesh Podcast by Dwarkesh Patel

For thought-provoking interviews and deep dives into the minds of AI innovators, look no further than the Dwarkesh Podcast by Dwarkesh Patel.

With over 30,000 subscribers, this podcast has built a reputation for engaging, in-depth conversations with some of the most influential figures in AI. From pioneers like Jeff Dean to rising stars in the field, Dwarkesh Patel’s interviews uncover fresh perspectives and innovative ideas that might just reshape the way you think about technology.

The strength of this podcast lies in its focus on the intellectual underpinnings of AI research. By exploring the stories behind breakthrough innovations, it offers listeners a nuanced understanding of both the challenges and the opportunities in the AI space. If you’re looking for interviews that challenge your thinking and inspire you to explore new ideas, this podcast should be at the top of your list.

▶️ Watch on YouTube | 🍎 Listen on Apple | 🎧 Listen on Spotify

#3. The AI Podcast by Nvidia

When you think about groundbreaking AI research and its practical applications, The AI Podcast by Nvidia is a must-listen. This podcast features in-depth interviews that explore how AI is transforming industries from healthcare to entertainment.

What sets this podcast apart is the quality of its guests. With experts from various sectors discussing everything from the technical intricacies of machine learning to the broader impact of AI on society, you’re guaranteed a comprehensive view of the field. It’s like having a front-row seat to the innovations that are defining our future.

For anyone serious about understanding how AI is reshaping our world, The AI Podcast by Nvidia offers the perfect blend of technical depth and accessible storytelling.

🍎 Listen on Apple | 🎧 Listen on Spotify

#4. The AI Daily Brief

Keeping up with AI can feel impossible when the news cycle changes by the hour. The AI Daily Brief is built for exactly that problem: a tight, daily news analysis show that helps you track the most important model releases, research breakthroughs, and debates shaping the industry.

Hosted by Nathaniel Whittemore, the show covers AI from multiple angles, including real-world business implications, ethics and policy, and the bigger questions around where all of this is heading.

Episodes are short enough to fit into a commute or a workout, but dense enough that you actually walk away feeling caught up.

▶️ Watch on YouTube | 🍎 Listen on Apple | 🎧 Listen on Spotify

#5. Last Week in AI

Keeping up with the latest in AI can feel like trying to catch lightning in a bottle. That’s why Last Week in AI is an absolute gem. Hosted by Andrey Kurenkov and Jeremie Harris, this bi-weekly roundup delivers a comprehensive summary of the most important AI news, innovations, and ethical debates from the previous week.

Each episode is like a pulse-check on the AI world—covering everything from breakthroughs in machine learning to pressing discussions about AI ethics and societal impacts. The hosts not only summarize the news but also provide insightful commentary on what these developments mean for the future. It’s the perfect podcast for anyone who wants to stay informed without having to scour endless articles and reports.

By tuning in, you’ll get a well-rounded view of the AI landscape, ensuring you never miss out on the trends that are shaping our world.

▶️ Watch on YouTube | 🍎 Listen on Apple | 🎧 Listen on Spotify

#6. AI For Humans: Making Artificial Intelligence Fun & Practical

AI For Humans truly lives up to its name. Artificial intelligence doesn’t have to be intimidating or overly technical.

Hosted by Gavin Purcell and Kevin Pereira, this podcast brings a refreshing blend of education and entertainment to the table. The show uses practical demonstrations, engaging stories, and a healthy dose of humor to make complex topics accessible.

Whether you’re a newbie trying to understand the basics or a seasoned professional looking for a light-hearted yet insightful discussion, AI For Humans has something for everyone. The hosts break down advanced concepts into everyday language, making it easier to grasp how AI impacts our lives and industries.

This podcast isn’t just about facts and figures—it’s about sparking curiosity and making learning fun. It’s a reminder that even the most cutting-edge technology can have a human touch.

▶️ Watch on YouTube | 🍎 Listen on Apple | 🎧 Listen on Spotify

#7 The Cognitive Revolution

If you want depth instead of headlines, The Cognitive Revolution is one of the best listens in AI right now.

Host Nathan Labenz “scouts AI from every angle” through long-form conversations with builders, researchers, and investors, plus his occasional “AI Scouting Reports” that synthesize complex topics into clear takeaways.

Labenz is the founder and former CEO of Waymark and brings a thoughtful, analytical lens (informed by his philosophy background) to how AI is reshaping work, society, and culture.

▶️ Watch on YouTube | 🍎 Listen on Apple | 🎧 Listen on Spotify

#8 No Priors

If you want founder-level signal on what matters in AI, No Priors is a solid pick.

Co-hosts Elad Gil and Sarah Guo talk with leading AI engineers, researchers, and company builders about the questions executives keep circling back to: how close we are to AGI, what markets are most exposed to disruption, and what’s actually happening at the research frontier.

The show strikes a nice balance between state-of-the-art research and real-world implications for commerce, culture, and society, making it especially useful when you want more than hype and headlines.

▶️ Watch on YouTube | 🍎 Listen on Apple | 🎧 Listen on Spotify

#9. Leveraging AI with Isar Meitis

Navigating the AI revolution can be daunting, especially if you’re coming from a non-technical background. That’s where Leveraging AI steps in. Hosted by Isar Meitis—a seasoned CEO with years of experience—the podcast is tailored for business professionals who want to integrate AI into their strategies for career and business growth.

This podcast offers practical tips and strategies to help you understand which AI tools can boost productivity, streamline operations, and even redefine business models. Isar doesn’t just throw around buzzwords; he provides actionable advice, drawing on real-life examples to illustrate how AI can be effectively harnessed. From ethical considerations to the nitty-gritty of day-to-day operations, his insights are a blend of strategic thinking and practical know-how.

If you’re looking to stay competitive in an ever-evolving marketplace, Leveraging AI is a goldmine of wisdom and guidance.

▶️ Watch on YouTube | 🍎 Listen on Apple | 🎧 Listen on Spotify

#10. Me, Myself and AI

Ever wonder why only a handful of companies truly succeed with AI? Me, Myself and AI gets to the core of that question. Hosted by Sam Ransbotham and Shervin Khodabandeh, this podcast isn’t just about technology—it’s about the human side of AI adoption.

It explores how organizations integrate AI into their daily operations and what it takes to overcome the unique challenges that come with it.

What I love about this podcast is its focus on real-world success stories. You’ll hear from leaders at renowned companies like NASA and Shopify who share candid insights about their AI journeys.

Their stories reveal how intelligent decision-making, strategic planning, and a willingness to experiment with new technology can transform business practices. It’s about understanding the keys to success and learning from both triumphs and setbacks.

If you’re curious about the practical side of AI—how it can solve everyday business challenges and drive growth—Me, Myself and AI offers a treasure trove of lessons. It’s like having a front-row seat to the future of work.

▶️ Watch on YouTube | 🍎 Listen on Apple | 🎧 Listen on Spotify

Tune In or Miss Out

In a world where AI is evolving faster than ever, these podcasts offer a unique blend of technical insights, real-world applications, and engaging conversations.

Whether you’re looking to empower yourself with the latest trends, boost your career, or simply enjoy a good story about how AI is changing everything, these top 10 shows have got you covered.

So, plug in, lean into the change, and let these podcasts be your guide to the fascinating, and sometimes unpredictable, world of artificial intelligence in 2026. Happy listening!
