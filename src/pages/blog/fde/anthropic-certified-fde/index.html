Standardizing on Claude changes the question

There is a moment in an enterprise AI journey when the model debate ends. You have evaluated the options, Claude has won the work that matters to you, and the team is committed. That decision quietly changes every question that follows. It is no longer "which model," a question generic AI advice is happy to answer. It is "how do we deploy this specific model, into these specific systems, safely and at production quality," which is a different and much more concrete kind of problem.

Generic help is the wrong shape for that problem. What you need is someone who knows Claude deeply, its agent capabilities, its tool use, how to build reliable retrieval around it, how to evaluate and govern it, and who can build inside your environment rather than advise from outside it. That combination, deep Claude expertise plus embedded production engineering, is exactly what an Anthropic-certified forward deployed engineer is for.

The gap between a Claude license and a Claude system

Having access to Claude is the starting line, not the finish. The distance between a capable model and a system your business depends on is made of specific, unglamorous engineering, and it is where teams without deep model expertise tend to get stuck.

Consider what a real Claude deployment actually requires. The model has to be connected to your data through retrieval that returns the right context reliably, not just most of the time. If you are building agents, they need tool access, guardrails around the steps that carry consequences, and orchestration that behaves predictably under load. You need an evaluation approach that tells you whether the system is doing its job correctly, run continuously rather than judged once at a demo. You need governance and monitoring that satisfy security and compliance. And all of it has to integrate with systems that were not designed with AI in mind. None of that is exotic, but all of it takes someone who has done it before on this model, which is precisely the gap a certified FDE closes and a generalist learns on your budget.
