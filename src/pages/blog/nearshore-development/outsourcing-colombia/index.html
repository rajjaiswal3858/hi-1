The Rise of Colombia as an Outsourcing Haven

Colombia is a country known for its quality coffee and glittering emeralds—two things they export regularly.

But the country’s natural goods aren’t all they have to offer. It turns out they’re rich in tech talent and services-based talent, making them an increasingly attractive outsourcing partner option for US businesses.

Just look at how dominant the services industry was for Colombia in the 2010s:

In 2019, nearly 63% of private sector employment in Colombia was related to the services industry. While the data doesn’t include the past few years, other sources indicate economic growth.

Statista’s prediction is that Colombia’s gross domestic product will increase by 27.52% between 2023 and 2028. When you consider that alongside the prevalence of the service industry (which will include the outsourcing sector), it’s easy to see that it’s driving much of that projected growth.

The country has also put an emphasis on education and training. They’ve even set a goal for themselves: to become the best-educated country in Latin America by 2025.

That determination will only increase interest in their skills from abroad and create a highly educated workforce over time.

Another reason for the rise in technology outsourcing to Colombia? Like the rest of Latin America, it’s cost-effective and convenient.

The shared or similar time zones make for effortless collaboration that further outsourcing destinations like Asia struggle with.

Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)

Business Process Outsourcing, refers to the practice where a company contracts out certain business operations to an external service provider. These outsourced services or processes typically include non-primary business activities that are essential but not part of the core business functions.

In 2021, Colombia led the Offshore BPO Confidence Index with a striking 90.9% rating. This confidence stems from Colombia’s strong infrastructure in telecommunications and IT, coupled with a skilled, cost-effective workforce.

These factors enable Colombian BPO services to offer high-quality, reliable solutions that align with global standards.

Call Center Services

Colombia ranks third in Latin America for call center services, a testament to its capabilities in this sector.

And when you think about it, using Colombia and other Latin American countries is a natural fit for call center services focused on the US. While English is our most widely spoken language, Spanish is second.

So if a Colombian call center employee is fluent in both, which they would be, then they can speak in whichever language is easiest for the person they’re calling.

Navigating Challenges in Outsourcing to Colombia

Outsourcing to Colombia, while advantageous, does present specific challenges. Addressing these effectively is crucial for a successful partnership.

Below, we’ve outlined three common issues US businesses can face when outsourcing to Colombia and how they can overcome those challenges.

They are:

  • Overcoming language barriers
  • Addressing infrastructure and connectivity
  • Avoiding geopolitical interruptions

Overcoming Language Barriers

English proficiency in Colombia has improved, especially in urban areas. However, understanding workplace jargon remains a hurdle.

To mitigate this, many Colombian outsourcing firms invest in language training, focusing not just on general proficiency but also on the specific linguistic needs of their industry.

This ensures that their workforce is not only bilingual but also familiar with the technical vocabulary and nuances of their clients’ businesses.

Companies outsourcing to Colombia should, however, remain mindful of potential cultural and language differences and nuances and maintain clear, jargon-free communication to ensure mutual understanding.

Addressing Infrastructure and Connectivity

Not every country has the robust internet infrastructure and wifi that the US does.

And in parts of Colombia, the most reliable internet connectivity may be patchy. This can interrupt projects and communication between teams especially since the tech industry is so reliant on digital tools.

The good news is that the country has invested in enhancing its internet connectivity and technological infrastructure which will better support the demands of the global outsourcing market.

These ongoing developments are vital in ensuring reliable and efficient operations, especially for technology-driven services like software development and the BPO sector.

Businesses looking to outsource to Colombia should stay informed about these infrastructural advancements to gauge the reliability and capability of their potential outsourcing partners.

Avoiding Geopolitical Interruptions

Like many countries, Colombia faces its own set of geopolitical challenges that can potentially impact outsourcing relationships and foreign investments.

Political stability, foreign investments, economic policies, competitive costs, and regulatory changes are factors that businesses must consider when outsourcing to any foreign country.

Building strong relationships with local partners who have a deep understanding of the Colombian market can provide valuable insights and help mitigate risks associated with geopolitical uncertainties.

At HatchWorks, we prevent disruptions to projects from geopolitical factors by sourcing talent from multiple LATAM countries. That way, if Colombian software engineers can’t continue a project, there are other developers on hand to pick it up.

Practical Steps to Outsource Software Development to Colombia

If outsourcing to Colombia feels like a natural fit for your business needs, there are a few things to consider to ensure success.

Identifying the Right Partners

Finding a reliable outsourcing company in Colombia is crucial. Start by researching the outsourcing company’s track record and client testimonials to gauge their reliability and quality of work.

Look for firms that have experience in your specific industry or with similar projects. Networking within industry circles and attending tech conferences can also provide valuable insights and contacts. You’ll also want to assess the company’s technological capabilities and whether they align with your project needs.

As we mentioned before, HatchWorks can do this for you. We’re US-based with teams across LatAm, including Colombia. We’ve recently been recognized as one of Colombia’s most reviewed B2B companies by The Manifest, showcasing our strong client relationships and high-quality service.

Legal and Compliance Considerations

When outsourcing to Colombia, it’s important to be aware of legal and compliance differences, especially in data protection laws.

Colombia’s data protection regulations may differ from those in your country, such as GDPR. Ensure that the outsourcing partner understands and can comply with the data protection requirements applicable to your business.

This might involve drafting clear agreements that specify how data should be handled and protected. Consulting with legal experts familiar with Colombian and international data protection laws can provide guidance and help mitigate risks.

Establishing Effective Communication Channels

Establish clear communication channels and a shared development process from the outset.

Utilize a variety of tools for different purposes – video conferencing for regular meetings, instant messaging for quick updates, and project management software for tracking progress and deliverables.

It’s also important to establish a regular meeting schedule that accommodates the time zone differences, ensuring that both teams can collaborate effectively.
