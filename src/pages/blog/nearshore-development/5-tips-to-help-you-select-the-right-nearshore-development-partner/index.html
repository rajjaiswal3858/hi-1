Nearshore software outsourcing services have become an increasingly popular way for companies to save money and increase their bottom line. But there is a lot of confusion about the differences between Onshore, Offshore, and Nearshore.

The digital landscape is changing at lightning speed. New technologies are emerging at a fast pace and your business could fall behind without the right partner to support your software development projects.

This brief guide will explain why Nearshore is an increasingly popular option and how to select the right Nearshore software development partner.

  • Why you should partner with a nearshore development company
  • What to look for in a nearshore software development partner
  • Learn more about Nearshore Software Development

Onshore means hiring software developers in the same country or region. Offshore indicates that the company you hired is in another country with a different time zone, like India. Nearshore refers to your outsourcing partner with resources in a neighboring country that is a short distance away and often in the same timezone, such as LATAM .”

Why you should partner with a nearshore development company

With the nearshore model of software development outsourcing, companies find their match in partners that are closer to home and offer lower costs than onshore through savings in logistics and general overhead.

But finding the right partner is not so straightforward. To succeed with your project, you need to consider several factors before collaborating with a partner.

What to look for in a nearshore software development partner

There are many factors that need to be considered when looking for a software development partner past better value for your investment and aligned timezone.

Make sure your partner has Errors and Omissions (E&O) Insurance. This is typical for US-based firms, but not necessarily for partners based in Latin America.
