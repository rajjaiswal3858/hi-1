Our Vision for the Future

At HatchWorks, we are committed to giving back to the rising generation of technologists in Georgia, Illinois, and Costa Rica. Hatch Futures is a professional development and volunteer program for empowering at-risk youth with the skills they need to build a brighter future. 

We have a widening income and education gap in the United States and many technology job opportunities are outsourced to other countries because we lack a fully trained workforce. Hatch Futures seeks to resolve this challenge and empower at-risk youth with hands-on technology training and mentoring across a variety of disciplines, career development and financial support for continued educational opportunities. 

Hatch Futures represents three philanthropic pillars to deliver on our promise to positively impact communities in Atlanta (Georgia), Chicago (Illinois), as well as San Jose, Costa Rica:

Summer Internships

Our six-week internship provides selected candidates with exposure to professional work environments and hands-on training for technology skills. 

Charitable Giving

We encourage charitable giving through clothing and food drives, fundraising, and donating—because no contribution is too small.

Volunteering

Our entire team dedicates 3+ days per year to volunteering at local organizations, and we encourage others to get involved by inviting our partners, customers, and families to volunteer with us.

Become a Valued Hatch Futures Partner
Hatch Futures partners with organizations including Girls Who Code, Year Up, and Bright Futures, to help with facilitating the growth and development of our programs. We currently provide financial support to each of these organizations. Hatch Futures offers portions of our company revenue to fund other organizations working with us to inspire and support the next generation of rising professionals.
