Our CEO, Brandon Powell, recently joined The Bliss Business Podcast for a live discussion on how HatchWorks AI has blended purpose with profit to drive meaningful impact.

Whether you’re a leader, entrepreneur, or changemaker, Brandon’s insights provide valuable guidance for aligning business success with a greater purpose.

Listen to the podcast

In this conversation, Brandon explored:

  • How businesses can balance financial goals with social impact
  • Real-world examples of companies succeeding with purpose-driven strategies
  • The role of social entrepreneurship in building resilient organizations

 

About Brandon Powell

Brandon Powell is the CEO of HatchWorks AI and an award-winning executive with a proven track record in tech leadership and social responsibility. A Harvard Business School alumnus, he’s been recognized as the 2023 Nearshore Entrepreneur of the Year, a 2020 Small Business Rockstar in Georgia, and a Gold Stevie Entrepreneur of the Year. Under his leadership, HatchWorks AI has emerged as a leader in AI transformation—connecting top software and data engineers in LATAM with U.S. companies. With successful ventures in international markets including Costa Rica and Colombia, Brandon further demonstrates his commitment to community impact through initiatives like HatchFutures.
