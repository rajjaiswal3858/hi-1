Our Journey

A foundational pivot, not a trend

Founded in 2016 by Brandon Powell and proudly based in Atlanta, Georgia, HatchWorks AI is a partner in AI and data transformation.

When generative AI, and ChatGPT in particular, burst onto the scene in November 2022, we felt two gut-level reactions.

Opportunity

This changes everything, creating limitless potential.

Urgency

Adapt quickly or face irrelevance.

AI had always been part of our toolkit, but this moment was different. It was a fundamental turning point, not just another trend. So we faced an existential fork in the road, and chose to pivot the entire company around generative AI. We dove in head-first, re-engineering our approach, retraining our teams, and refocusing our offerings. The transformation wasn't superficial; it was foundational.

It is easy to do AI.
It is hard to do well.

That's where we come in. We act as a guide, so you keep clarity in your strategy, control over your direction, and complete ownership of your IP, sidestepping the common pitfalls along the way.

What we believe

The gap between what AI can do and what is actually adopted is wide. We exist to close it through the power of your data.

We are a pure-play AI partner, not a generalist firm. We are not a legacy dev shop retrofitting AI, we are built around it.

A proven approach

Outcome driven by design
