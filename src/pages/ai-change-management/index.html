The gap

AI only creates value when people use it.

Launching an AI product, agent, or workflow doesn't guarantee adoption. People need to understand what is changing, trust the new way of working, and know how it helps them succeed.

Without clear ownership, enablement, measurement, and guardrails, adoption stalls—and the expected value never materializes.

The GenEQ model

Make adoption part of the system—not an afterthought.

GenEQ brings together three capabilities required to create lasting change.

01

Change management

Align leaders, prepare stakeholders, redesign affected workflows, and give each audience the support needed to adopt new AI capabilities.

02

Observability

Establish an adoption baseline, define meaningful KPIs, and track where new behaviors are taking hold—or where progress is falling behind.

03

Governance

Set clear ownership, decision rights, policies, and guardrails so adoption can scale responsibly across teams and use cases.

Training and enablement may support the plan, but they are not the plan. The goal is sustained behavior change and measurable adoption.

How GenEQ works

Adoption starts before launch.

GenEQ supports the change from early planning through rollout and ongoing improvement.

01

Assess the change

Understand the people, roles, workflows, and business units affected by the new AI capability.

02

Design the adoption plan

Define stakeholders, success measures, communications, enablement, governance, and the actions required to drive adoption.

03

Activate the organization

Engage leaders, champions, managers, and end users while integrating AI into the workflows where work happens.

04

Measure and adapt

Monitor adoption and value signals, identify gaps, and adjust the plan as the organization learns.
