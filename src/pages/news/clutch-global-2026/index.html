HatchWorks AI: Clutch Global 2026 Leader in Three AI Categories

  • Andy Smith
  • September 9, 2026
  • Updated: October 16, 2026

The AI services firm was recognized in AI Deployment, AI Strategy, and Machine Learning — placing it among the top 15 companies worldwide in each.

ATLANTA — July 30, 2026 — HatchWorks AI, a pure-play AI partner that helps enterprises turn AI into measurable ROI, today announced it has been named a Summer 2026 Clutch Global Award winner in three categories: AI Deployment, AI Strategy, and Machine Learning.

Clutch, the leading global marketplace of B2B service providers, selects honorees through its proprietary Ability to Deliver methodology, which evaluates companies on verified client feedback, project success, industry expertise, and overall market presence. Clutch Global Awards represent the highest level of recognition on the platform, reserved for the top 15 companies in each category worldwide.

HatchWorks AI is one of more than 400 companies recognized across 54 IT and software development categories this cycle — and one of a small number honored in three separate AI disciplines. The three categories map closely to how the company works with clients: setting AI strategy, deploying systems into production, and building the machine learning that powers them.

"Winning in strategy, deployment, and machine learning at the same time says something specific about how we work. Plenty of firms can write an AI strategy deck. Fewer can ship the system it describes and keep it running in production. This recognition comes from verified client reviews, which means it reflects what our clients actually experienced — not what we say about ourselves."

Brandon Powell CEO and Founder, HatchWorks AI

"Behind every successful digital transformation is a trusted technology partner. Our Summer 2026 Clutch Global winners have distinguished themselves by delivering innovative solutions and consistently strong outcomes for their clients."

Mike Beares Founder and CEO, Clutch

The recognition follows a run of 2025–2026 honors for HatchWorks AI, including designation as an Inc. Power Partner in AI and software development for the third consecutive year and inclusion among the Top 100 Enterprise Software Development Companies. Founded in 2016 and headquartered in Atlanta, the company operates across the Americas with teams in the United States, Costa Rica, Colombia, and Peru, and holds partnerships with Anthropic, Google Cloud, Databricks, and OpenAI.

HatchWorks AI currently holds a 4.9 out of 5 rating on Clutch across 29 verified client reviews.
