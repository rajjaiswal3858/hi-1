HatchWorks AI Wins Inc. Power Partner Award For 3rd Year

  • Andy Smith
  • October 20, 2026
  • Updated: October 20, 2026

The annual Inc. Power Partner Awards recognize B2B companies that provide essential services and expertise to help entrepreneurs and businesses grow

ATLANTA, GA, October 20, 2026: HatchWorks AI, an AI-native software development firm that helps companies turn AI into measurable business results, has been named a 2026 Inc. Power Partner Awards winner, recognizing the company among the leading B2B partners supporting entrepreneurs and businesses across the country.

The annual Inc. Power Partner Awards honor B2B companies that have demonstrated a track record of helping entrepreneurs and businesses navigate the challenges of building and growing a company. From hiring and compliance to technology, infrastructure, fundraising, marketing, and more, these partners provide expertise and services that enable businesses to operate, scale, and pursue their ambitions.

“Business owners have no shortage of tools at their disposal, and B2B companies need to make clear why their products and services are worth the investment,” says Bonny Ghosh, editorial director at Inc. “The companies on this year’s Power Partners list have done just that. They don’t just have customers – they have superfans and evangelists. Across industries like marketing, financial services, and technology, they’ve proved their dedication to helping fellow business owners grow and succeed.”

“Being named an Inc. Power Partner for a third time means the most because it comes from our clients. They tell Inc. whether we delivered. Our job is to help them turn AI into real business results, and we are grateful they trust us to do it.”

Brandon Powell CEO and Founder, HatchWorks AI

HatchWorks AI was recognized in the AI, Technology & Development category for helping enterprises move AI out of the pilot stage and into production. Its teams build AI strategies and roadmaps tied to business priorities, create AI-ready data foundations, and develop AI-powered products, applications, and agents. Clients scale that work with HatchWorks AI engineering teams in the US and Latin America.

That delivery runs on Generative-Driven Development (GenDD), the HatchWorks AI operating model for building software with AI. GenDD defines how engineers and AI agents split work across the full development lifecycle. Human review, quality gates, and delivery metrics are built in, so teams ship faster without losing control of quality. GenDD earned HatchWorks AI the Code Generative AI Solution of the Year title in the 2026 AI Breakthrough Awards.

This is the third time HatchWorks AI has been named an Inc. Power Partner, following recognition in 2023 and 2025. Other recent honors include the Dallas Innovates AI 75 list for Brandon Powell, induction into the Georgia Titan 100 Hall of Fame in 2026, and a Fall 2025 Clutch Global Award for AI and software development.

For more information on Inc.’s Power Partner Awards or to view the complete list of honorees, visit https://www.inc.com/power-partner-awards.
