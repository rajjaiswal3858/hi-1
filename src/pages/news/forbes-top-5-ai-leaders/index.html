HatchWorks AI Leaders Recognized Among Forbes Top 5 AI Leaders Bringing AI to Everyone

  • Andy Smith
  • August 28, 2025
  • Updated: September 4, 2025

ATLANTA — August 25, 2025 — HatchWorks AI is proud to announce that CEO and Founder Brandon Powell and Board Member Rajeev Kapur have been recognized in Forbes’ article, Top 5 AI Leaders Bringing Artificial Intelligence to Everyone by Michael Ashley.

The feature highlights global thought leaders making AI practical, accessible, and transformative across industries and communities.

Brandon Powell: Championing Human-Centered AI

As CEO of HatchWorks AI, Brandon Powell has led the company to be named an Inc. Power Partner in AI & Software Development and recognized as a #1 Gen AI Solution Provider.

Forbes spotlighted Powell’s philosophy as an AI humanist, emphasizing that AI’s true impact lies in enabling everyday people to unlock new potential and productivity:

AI’s real value emerges when everyday people—not just engineers—use it to do more with less, execute new ideas, and transform their work. When AI becomes as natural as flipping a light switch, we’ll see a true shift, not just in productivity, but in human potential.”

Brandon Powell, CEO and Founder of HatchWorks AI

Powell also underscored the importance of ensuring people are not left behind in the AI journey, highlighting the need for training, trust, and thoughtful change management as organizations scale AI adoption.

Rajeev Kapur: Democratizing Opportunity Through AI

HatchWorks AI Board Member Rajeev Kapur, bestselling author of AI Made Simple and Prompting Made Simple, was also recognized by Forbes for his global leadership in translating AI into actionable strategies.

Through his work as CEO of 1105 Media and founder of the Kapur Center for AI Leadership, he is committed to equipping leaders, educators, and communities worldwide with the tools to thrive in the AI era.

AI isn’t here to replace people—it’s here to replace tasks. The real winners will be those who combine human judgment, creativity and empathy with AI’s speed, accuracy and scale.”

Rajeev Kapur, Board Member, HatchWorks AI

Kapur emphasized AI’s role in democratizing education and entrepreneurship, enabling students and small businesses around the world to access the same resources as large institutions and corporations.

Recognition of Impact and Vision

The inclusion of both Powell and Kapur in Forbes’ Top 5 AI Leaders underscores HatchWorks AI’s growing influence as a driver of practical, human-centered AI innovation.

Together, their leadership reflects the company’s mission to make AI adoption both accessible and impactful—helping enterprises across industries deliver measurable outcomes while expanding opportunity for communities worldwide.
