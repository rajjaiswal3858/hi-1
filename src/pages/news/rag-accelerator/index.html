HatchWorks AI Launches RAG Accelerator

  • Andy Smith
  • August 27, 2024
  • Updated: August 4, 2025

Create AI-Driven Insights from Your Proprietary Data

Atlanta, GA – August 20, 2024 –- HatchWorks AI is excited to introduce the RAG Accelerator, a cutting-edge solution that helps business turn their data into a differntiator.

Traditional AI models often fall short in delivering accurate, context-specific insights due to their lack of access to your structured and unstrutured data. Our RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) Accelerator bridges this gap by securely leveraging your proprietary information, enabling precise and relevant AI responses through natural language.

With the RAG Accelerator, organizations can unlock smarter decision-making, boost productivity, and ensure their data remains secure. This solution augments AI’s generative capabilities with the unique, contextual information from your own data, ensuring AI-driven outputs are both relevant and accurately aligned with your business needs.

Our RAG Accelerator meets the critical demand for precise, context-specific AI responses. By securely leveraging proprietary data, this service not only enhances productivity it can also be enabled in companies customer facing products to create unique experiences.

"The launch of our RAG Accelerator is a game-changer for organizations across a variety of industries looking to tap into the full potential of their proprietary data. By combining generative AI with the unique context of a company’s structured and unstructured data, we deliver AI-driven insights that are more accurate and actionable. This technology unlocks a wide range of possibilities, from enhancing customer support to transforming operations in healthcare and finance."

Brandon Powell, CEO and Founder of HatchWorks AI
