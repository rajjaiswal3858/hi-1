Proprietary Patient Management System Unlocks 99% Faster Implementation and Client Onboarding

  • Andy Smith
  • February 8, 2025
  • Updated: August 4, 2025

Patient Management Systems are taking a giant leap forward with the launch of Charter, a proprietary patient management system (PMS) designed to transform how specialty pharmacy programs operate, scale, and deliver patient-centered care.

Developed within 11 months, Charter leverages the power of Amazon Web Services (AWS) to streamline clinical workflows, accelerate client onboarding by up to 99%, and unlock actionable insights for improved patient outcomes.

Addressing a Critical Industry Gap

Traditional patient management solutions, including legacy platforms like Salesforce, often require healthcare organizations to adapt their workflows to the tool rather than the other way around.

This “tool-first” mindset has historically stifled innovation, fragmented data, and required manual workarounds that impede clinical efficiency and timely patient engagement.

When we relied on a simpler system, we had to bend our workflows to fit the system. We lost time and the ability to strategically connect patient metrics to specific therapies. We needed a purpose-built solution that could evolve with us, rather than force us into rigid structures. My optimism and confidence in choosing Hatchworks AI as a partner has been proven. Their ability to become an extension of our team and partner with us to deliver Charter has been uniquely successful because of their team and their talent. It’s been something special.”

Rusty Atkinson, Vice President of Technology at Clearway Health

A Purpose-Built, Patient-First Solution

Charter emerged from a close collaboration between Clearway Health and HatchWorks AI to architect, design, and deliver a PMS aligned with real-world clinical and operational needs. By leveraging AWS, the platform ensures long-term scalability, cost-efficiency, and integration with existing enterprise applications.

Key Features & Benefits:

  • 99% Faster Implementation and Client Onboarding: The new platform dramatically reduces the time to bring new clients online, improving operational agility and allowing Clearway Health to rapidly scale services.
  • Logical Data Schema: Charter unifies and enriches patient data and ensures data integrity, reduces redundancy and improves performance. This allows for an improved end user experience and strengthened data to drive business insights.
  • Enhanced User Experience: The intuitive interface guides specialty pharmacy teams to high-priority tasks, reducing manual data entry and paperwork. The result is more time spent on patient care, improving both patient and clinician satisfaction.
  • Future-Ready Infrastructure: Built on AWS, Charter’s modular architecture paves the way for continuous innovation. The platform can easily incorporate new features, integrations, and analytics capabilities, ensuring Clearway Health remains at the forefront of specialty pharmacy care.

Charter isn’t just a piece of software—it’s a critical enabler of our vision to eliminate barriers to medication access and improve patient health. By aligning technology with our team’s needs and patients’ expectations, we’re raising the bar for what a specialty pharmacy program can achieve.”

HatchWorks AI led the product discovery, architecture design, and iterative development of Charter. The process included collaborative workshops, cross-functional stakeholder engagement, and continuous feedback loops to ensure the platform aligned with Clearway Health’s evolving needs.

From the start, we knew this wasn’t just a technology project—it was about empowering Clearway Health to deliver optimal patient care.
