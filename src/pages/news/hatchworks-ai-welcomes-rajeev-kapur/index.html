HatchWorks AI Welcomes Rajeev Kapur to Board of Directors

  • Andy Smith
  • June 15, 2025
  • Updated: August 4, 2025

Renowned AI Leader, Multi-Time CEO, and Bestselling Author Joins to Support Strategic Growth and AI Innovation

Atlanta, GA – HatchWorks AI is proud to announce that Rajeev Kapur has joined its Board of Directors. A global business leader, bestselling author, and AI advisor, Rajeev brings over 30 years of executive experience scaling organizations and driving innovation across industries.

Rajeev’s background includes a standout tenure at Dell where he built a $1B+ business in the U.S. and led the company’s China and South Asia divisions to record growth.

He has since served as CEO and President of multiple mid-sized companies, delivering three successful exits.

Most recently, he was named a finalist for the 2024 Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year Award and has been recognized as one of Orange County’s Top 500 Executives.

Rajeev is also a dual-certified AI expert from MIT and the bestselling author of Chase Greatness and AI Made Simple: A Beginner’s Guide to Generative Intelligence, which released its second edition in June 2024.

His speaking engagements have taken him around the world, and he was the highest-rated presenter at the YPO EDGE Barcelona conference for his keynote “A Taste of AI.” He currently sits on two other AI boards and serves as an advisor to global brands including Samsung and Hugo Boss.

In joining the HatchWorks AI board, Rajeev will provide strategic guidance as the company continues expanding its leadership in AI-driven software development and accelerates the impact of its proprietary approaches like Generative-Driven Development™.
