HatchWorks AI Named a Top B2B Company for AI and Software Development Services

Atlanta, GA, June 10, 2024 –- HatchWorks AI, a leading AI development partner, is honored to be recognized as a 2024 Spring Clutch Global Award winner. 

We’re proud to be recognized as a leader in AI and Software Development on a global scale. This award is a testament to the excellent client work we have delivered this year as recognized through the voice of our customers in their reviews on Clutch.

“This recognition from Clutch validates our commitment to delivering top-tier AI solutions and software development services that empower our clients to achieve their goals,” said Brandon Powell, CEO of HatchWorks AI and a 2024 Titan 100 honoree. “We are grateful for the trust our clients place in us and will continue to push the boundaries of innovation in AI and software development.”

Clutch Global Awards showcase the very best in the B2B services industry worldwide. Honorees are selected based on their industry expertise and ability to deliver scores calculated from client feedback in thousands of reviews published on Clutch.

This year, HatchWorks AI has achieved several notable milestones. The company secured SOC 2 Type I compliance, marking a significant validation of HatchWorks’ internal controls and systems designed to protect customer data and handle sensitive information with the utmost security. Additionally, HatchWorks received a strategic growth capital investment from J Schwan, which supports the company’s ongoing growth and leadership in innovative technology delivery and includes the expansion of their Chicago office.

HatchWorks also launched Generative-Driven Development™, a cutting-edge approach that leverages generative AI to accelerate and streamline the software development lifecycle, leading to a 30-50% productivity increase for HatchWorks’ clients. This methodology has positioned HatchWorks as a frontrunner in the field of generative AI, earning recognition such as the Inc Power Partner Award in Software Development and AI, and being named Top Machine Learning Company and Top AI Company by Clutch.
