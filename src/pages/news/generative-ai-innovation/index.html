HatchWorks Leads the Charge in AI-Powered Software Development

ATLANTA, GA, January 19, 2024 – In a remarkable year marked by technological advancements, HatchWorks has emerged as a frontrunner in the field of generative AI, earning recognition for its contributions to the software development industry.

Throughout 2023, HatchWorks garnered accolades such as the Inc Power Partner Award in the categories of Software Development and Artificial Intelligence (A.I) & Data and was named Top Machine Learning Company and Top AI Company by Clutch.

These awards highlight not just the company’s position as a leader in AI-powered software development but also its dedication to reshaping the technology landscape with its unique Generative-Driven Development™ approach.

This methodology leverages generative AI to enhance efficiency and reduce costs, leading to a 30-50% productivity increase for our clients and setting a new standard in the industry.

In a recent client project, HatchWorks streamlined compliance processes using AI. This project highlights HatchWorks’ commitment and success in transforming complex tasks into efficient, AI-driven operations.

Taryn Owen, President & CEO of TrueBlue, emphasizes HatchWorks’ significant role in their software development evolution. “We have trusted HatchWorks with our most strategic development projects for over five years. Their Nearshore model, combined with their AI capabilities, has been a game-changer for our software development practice,” says Owen.

As HatchWorks continues to pave the way in AI innovation, their achievements are a clear indicator of their potential to drive future advancements in the field.

HatchWorks’ journey in 2023 and beyond is a story of setting new standards and exploring uncharted territories in the ever-evolving world of generative AI.
