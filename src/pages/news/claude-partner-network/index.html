HatchWorks AI Joins the Claude Partner Network to Put Claude Into Production for Enterprises

  • Updated: November 10, 2026

The Atlanta-based AI firm embeds Forward Deployed Engineers who hold Claude certifications inside enterprise teams to find where Claude delivers value, ship it into production, and make adoption stick.

HatchWorks AI is Certified in the Claude Partner Network. The Claude Partner Network is Anthropic's program for firms that put Claude into production for customers. It's a natural step for HatchWorks AI, the pure-play AI firm behind Generative-Driven Development (GenDD), whose work is about getting Claude out of pilots and into production.

The company's model centers on Forward Deployed Engineers: senior AI builders who embed inside client teams, find where Claude delivers measurable value, ship it into production, and train the organization to sustain it. The premise is simple. Access to Claude is not the same as results from Claude. HatchWorks AI exists to close that gap.

Access Is Not Adoption

Most enterprise AI initiatives stall between evaluation and production. Licenses get bought. Pilots run. Results do not scale. The cause is rarely the technology. It is the absence of people who can absorb Claude into how the business actually works.

Every enterprise can buy Claude. Far fewer can put it into production. The shift required is behavioral, not just technical: new ways of working, new disciplines, and new muscle most companies do not have in-house.

The Model: Forward Deployed Engineers

A Forward Deployed Engineer is a senior AI builder who works inside the business instead of advising from the outside. Each one holds Claude certifications and runs a three-phase model:

  • Find. The FDE embeds with the team, learns how the business runs, and identifies where Claude delivers measurable ROI.
  • Ship. They build Claude-powered systems tailored to the client's operations, governed and evaluated for production.
  • Multiply. They train executives, enable teams, and lead the change management that turns Claude from a tool into a behavior.

HatchWorks offers three ways to bring Claude in: individual Forward Deployed Engineers for teams ready to ship, Agentic AI Pods that own Claude outcomes across a full product, and End-to-End Transformation for organizations treating AI as an enterprise-wide priority. In every model, HatchWorks owns the outcome, measured by production AI in the business rather than pilots or prototypes.

Putting Claude into production is where the real value shows up for our customers. Enterprises do not have an AI access problem. They have an AI adoption problem. Joining the Claude Partner Network lets us scale the work of closing that gap.

Brandon Powell, CEO, HatchWorks AI

Built on GenDD

Every HatchWorks engagement runs on Generative-Driven Development, the company's proprietary methodology for building with AI. GenDD defines a repeatable division of labor between humans, AI, and accumulated context, so engagements are engineered rather than improvised. It is what lets FDE work move fast and stay low-risk at the same time. A full breakdown is available in the GenDD eBook.

Availability

HatchWorks AI is accepting enterprise engagements now. Organizations interested in putting Claude into production can learn more at hatchworks.com/anthropic. Details on the program are available on the Claude Partner Network program page and in Anthropic's Services Track launch announcement.
