HatchWorks Secures SOC 2 Type I Compliance: Top Data Standards

  • Andy Smith
  • May 2, 2024
  • Updated: August 4, 2025
Atlanta, GA – May 2, 2024 –- HatchWorks announced today that it has achieved SOC 2 Type I compliance in accordance with American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) standards for SOC for Service Organizations also known as SSAE 18.

This certification marks a significant validation by an external auditor of HatchWorks’ internal controls and systems designed to protect customer data and handle sensitive information with the utmost security.

The SOC 2 report, focusing on security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy, offers HatchWorks’ business partners and clients reinforced confidence in the company’s ability to manage data securely and effectively.

HatchWorks achieved a favorableopinion in the SOC 2 Type I audit, underscoring its operational effectiveness and adherence to the trust services criteria.

This achievement not only demonstrates compliance with established cybersecurity frameworks but also positions HatchWorks as a trusted software development and AI services provider committed to protecting sensitive data and intellectual property against security breaches and data breaches.

As cybersecurity compliance frameworks continue to evolve, HatchWorks is dedicated to maintaining a proactive approach in its security posture and compliance program.

The company’s ongoing efforts to implement controls and demonstrate compliance through regular SOC reports are integral to its strategy to provide assurance and transparency to its clients and other stakeholders.

For our clients, the SOC 2 Type I compliance is not just a legal requirement but a commitment to maintaining a secure and reliable service environment.
