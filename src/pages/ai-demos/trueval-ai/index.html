TrueVal AI

TrueVal AI is an AI-powered real estate appraisal system that detects inconsistencies in appraisals, validates property comps, and generates a full fraud-risk assessment. It’s fully multimodal, featuring a voice agent you can interact with to ask questions about the property or the appraisal findings — even while you’re driving to a showing.

With an estimated $27B in annual losses and roughly 15% of U.S. appraisals showing signs of overvaluation, TrueVal AI helps bring transparency, speed, and trust to a market that urgently needs it.
