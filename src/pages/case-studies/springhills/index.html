Overview

The foundation of the care team's workflow

In 2020, Spring Hills engaged HatchWorks to define, design, and develop a new population health software platform that would act as the foundation of its care team's workflow, and scale with the organization's business needs over time.

The Challenge

A growing care team, managed in spreadsheets

Spring Hills' population health program sought to define the patient assessments and care team member workflows necessary to positively impact patient outcomes. Assessment write-ups and care team tasks were initially managed in spreadsheets and static documents.

As the care team grew, static documents were not sustainable. A more automated system was needed to manage patient information and clinical tasks.

The Process

From in-person workshops to a branded design system

HatchWorks designed and built the population health solution to support configurable workflows based on user roles and medical scenarios, and on different types of health system customers and patient care periods.

  1. 01

    In-person workshops

    The engagement kicked off with workshops defining the business case and market opportunity, and understanding the unique needs of nursing staff throughout patient management.

  2. 02

    Proof of concept, roadmap, and prototype

    HatchWorks created a proof of concept, a product roadmap, wireframes, and an interactive prototype, interviewing stakeholders to define the targeted users and their critical tasks.

  3. 03

    Workflows become the product logic

    Comprehensive user workflows and prototypes became the basis for the logic behind the task-generating functionality and the UX.

  4. 04

    Refined UX and a branded design system

    The UX was refined with additional wireframes and a branded design system was created, setting the stage for engineering to continue development of the MVP.

The Outcome

Launched in September 2020, spreadsheets retired the same day

The workflows helped Spring Hills refine internal processes to become more efficient. The design system established a comprehensive UX and clean interface, setting the stage for HatchWorks Engineering teams to continue development of the MVP.

The application launched in September 2020, and Spring Hills immediately transitioned from spreadsheet patient management to a custom application managing tasks and patient assessments.

To date, the application has reduced the 90 day readmission rate for the population that Spring Hills manages to under 10%.
