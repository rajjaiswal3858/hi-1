Overview

One design system, many solutions

HatchWorks was engaged by Diebold Nixdorf to redesign an existing solution that enables financial institutions to create and deliver marketing content to ATM machines.

The goal was a flexible, reusable UI design system that could be used to update multiple existing solutions across the Diebold Nixdorf portfolio.

The Challenge

A feature that grew into an interface, still tied to the legacy platform

The current solution began as a feature of a legacy software platform and evolved over time into its own separate interface. Its strong ties to the legacy platform created a rigid, overly complex, difficult to understand user experience.

Rigid

Constrained by the legacy platform it grew out of.

Overly complex

Years of accretion, not a designed experience.

Hard to understand

Marketing users struggled with core tasks.

The Process

Prioritize for core users, then design the system underneath

  1. 01

    Identify what matters most

    HatchWorks worked with the Product team to identify and prioritize the features most essential and valuable to core users.

  2. 02

    Match the workflows of marketing users

    User workflows were created to match the work processes of marketing users within financial institutions.

  3. 03

    Build a flexible design system

    The design system incorporated brand standards, complex interactions, and accessibility standards, flexible and scalable enough to become the basis for UI revisions to other solutions in the portfolio.

The Outcome

A new experience for the marketing application, and a system for the rest

HatchWorks delivered a comprehensive design system based in the Bootstrap framework, covering individual components such as buttons and menus and complex components such as table structures and modal layouts.

The system was used to define an entirely new experience for the marketing application, focused on enhancing core user activities while streamlining automation of marketing asset creation. Customers who volunteered to test the new design shared extremely positive feedback.
