Overview

Helping hardworking Americans reach financial independence, faster

Capital Choice Financial Group, founded in 1995, is dedicated to helping hardworking Americans achieve financial independence. Through a vast network of independent sales associates, it offers life insurance and annuity solutions alongside financial guidance.

As the company grew, legacy processes, specifically manual insurance quoting, were slowing down efficiency. Capital Choice partnered with HatchWorks to build a next-generation sales enablement platform that enhances client onboarding and financial planning.

The Challenge

Quoting by pen and paper

Legacy processes were holding the company back. Insurance quoting traditionally relied on manual, pen-and-paper calculations, which slowed sales associates and created inefficiencies in client onboarding.

Capital Choice needed a modern, intuitive platform to streamline quoting, enhance the customer experience, and empower associates to sell financial products faster and smarter.

Manual quoting

Pen-and-paper calculations for every quote.

Slow onboarding

A growing network without modern tools to sell with.

The Solution

MyCapitalChoice: quoting and financial modeling on any device

Through stakeholder workshops and rapid prototyping, HatchWorks developed MyCapitalChoice, a scalable, responsive platform for seamless quoting and financial modeling across devices.

The Outcome

A full financial picture in minutes

Associates can now provide clients with a full financial picture in minutes, demonstrating the role of insurance in future financial stability. Built on AWS for scalability and security, the platform gives Capital Choice long-term flexibility.
