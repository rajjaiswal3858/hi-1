Overview

An eCommerce overhaul built for peak season

HatchWorks partnered with a renowned North American children's apparel retailer to overhaul their eCommerce platform. The engagement aimed at reducing technical debt and leveraging the Salesforce Composable Storefront to upgrade the retailer's digital presence.

It included feature enhancements specifically designed to improve conversion rates and prepare the platform for peak shopping periods.

This case study is presented in a white-labeled format to comply with nondisclosure and privacy agreements. Specific details have been generalized to maintain confidentiality.

The Challenge

Outdated technology and a team in constant turnover

The retailer struggled with outdated technology and a high turnover of staff, affecting its ability to meet modern eCommerce demands. It needed a specialized approach that tackled technical debt and staffing strategies together.

HatchWorks was engaged for its expertise in React development, building dynamic, responsive user interfaces and seamless, interactive web and mobile applications. A new design system was implemented using Chakra UI, enhancing the aesthetics and usability of the mobile and web interfaces.

Technical debt

Outdated technology that could not keep up with modern eCommerce demands.

Staffing instability

High turnover that made continuity and delivery hard to sustain.

The Process

A dedicated team, a Salesforce upgrade, and a new PWA

  1. 01

    Integrate a dedicated team

    Senior and mid-level developers, a senior SDET, and a US-based engineering lead, all seasoned in Agile practices, integrated effectively with the client's existing teams.

  2. 02

    Upgrade Salesforce

    The Salesforce upgrade was pivotal in transforming the platform into a more dynamic and scalable environment.

  3. 03

    Launch the next-generation PWA

    A next-generation progressive web application framework complemented the upgrade, providing flexibility during the peak holiday shopping season.

The Outcome

Performance up, conversion up, and a stable IT organization

The engagement produced significant performance improvements and enhanced customer engagement. The Salesforce Composable Storefront and the PWA 2.0 launch were pivotal.

The impact of our Salesforce upgrade and PWA 2.0 launch: conversion rate climbed from a 3% baseline to 16.7% on Cyber Monday.
