Overview

A care management system that adapts to the workflow

HatchWorks partnered with Integrated Care Solutions (ICS), a startup created by National HealthCare Associates, to design and develop a flexible post-acute care management system. The system sought to help their care management team with workflows targeted at improving patient outcomes related to readmissions and length of stay in care facilities.

ICS needed a highly functional, automated, and customized solution that could adapt to unique workflows and varying customer contract needs.

The Challenge

Three user types, many overlapping workflows, one evolving organization

As a new startup with an evolving organization and care management approach, discovery and alignment were critical. HatchWorks facilitated a workshop with key stakeholders to define user needs and outline how features could enable team workflows to impact patient results.

The system needed to support several user types, and the many overlapping workflows were mapped and prioritized according to the dependencies between them.

The Process

Prototype, test with real nurses, then code

  1. 01

    Continuous stakeholder evaluation

    HatchWorks engaged with ICS's executives and stakeholders to continually evaluate business needs and incorporate feedback into each iteration.

  2. 02

    User interviews as the team grew

    As ICS hired more nursing staff, user interviews gathered usability feedback during ongoing development.

  3. 03

    Low-fidelity to high-fidelity prototypes

    Several low-fidelity prototypes were iterated, then a high-fidelity prototype was created to outline the core workflows.

  4. 04

    A coded MVP

    The validated prototype supported development of the coded MVP, which nurse teams then migrated to.

The Outcome

Prototypes that sold, trained, and de-risked the build

Validated UX workflows and UI designs, documented through clickable prototypes, gave ICS a comprehensive view of the product while MVP development progressed. The prototypes also served as a business development tool for prospective customers and investors, and as a training tool for onboarding new nurses.

Once the MVP was developed and nurse teams migrated, ICS documented measurable outcomes in cost of care, length of stay, and readmissions.
