Overview

Reimagine reporting from the ground up, but start by listening

Before engaging with HatchWorks, Juice Plus+ understood that their existing sales reporting system was not meeting the needs of their independent sales partners. They wanted to help partners grow and succeed, and saw an opportunity to reimagine reporting from the ground up, but were unsure where to focus the internal Product team's limited bandwidth.

As a critical first step, Juice Plus+ partnered with HatchWorks to gain a deeper understanding of partners' day-to-day wants, needs, and challenges.

The Challenge

Rigid, antiquated reporting that partners had outgrown

The ways in which Juice Plus+ partners interacted with their vital business data in the existing sales reporting system were rigid and antiquated. Presentations of data were confusing and prone to misinterpretation, and workflows were complicated and riddled with time-consuming workarounds.

Partners' expectations of how something should work had shifted through routine interactions with modern software.

The Process

Evaluate, interview, then survey at scale

  1. 01

    Evaluate the existing system

    HatchWorks analyzed the existing product materials and performed a high-level evaluation of the existing system.

  2. 02

    Qualitative interviews around the world

    The team composed, planned, and facilitated qualitative one-on-one interviews with partners from around the world, plus internal business stakeholders and subject matter experts.

  3. 03

    An evergreen quantitative survey

    HatchWorks helped the Product team develop a flexible quantitative survey framework, easily adapted and distributed to thousands of partners in various international markets, as an evergreen data source that outlives the project.

The Outcome

A clear path for a Product team with limited bandwidth

The research produced a wealth of observations, insights, and data, synthesized into high-level product recommendations and considerations. Low-fidelity wireframes supported new and enhanced product concepts and user flows.

Detailed findings and recommendations were presented to key stakeholders, with a project read-out document as the takeaway. The work gave Juice Plus+ direction on where to focus next, fostered buy-in on next steps, and provided a clear path to prioritizing ongoing work and bandwidth.
