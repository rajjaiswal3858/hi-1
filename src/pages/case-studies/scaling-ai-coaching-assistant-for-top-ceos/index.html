Overview

From a promising proof of concept to a production AI agent

CEO Coaching International works with some of the world's most impactful CEOs to help them lead and grow with precision. With AI playing a growing role in executive enablement, the firm set out to evolve "Eve," its AI-powered coaching assistant.

Partnering with HatchWorks AI, they took Eve from a promising proof of concept to a secure, scalable AI agent that now actively supports hundreds of high-level coaching sessions every month.

The Challenge

A working prototype that could not carry the weekly session volume

CEO Coaching International had built a working PoC of Eve, the AI-powered coaching assistant. While promising, the prototype wasn't scalable or robust enough to support the volume of high-level coaching sessions conducted weekly across their network.

The Process

Upgrade the architecture, then build intelligence into every session

  1. 01

    Upgrading the architecture

    HatchWorks AI redesigned Eve's system architecture to support enterprise-grade security, performance, and scalability, ensuring the platform could meet the needs of CEO Coaching International's elite clientele.

  2. 02

    Building intelligence into every session

    The upgraded Eve now joins coaching calls automatically, capturing critical data points, identifying key trends, and summarizing discussion topics into concise, secure post-session reports.

  3. 03

    Enhancing coaching value

    Beyond supporting the CEO, Eve also provides feedback to coaches, helping them fine-tune their approach and deliver maximum value across engagements.
