Overview

Bridging the communication gap between doctors and patients, at scale

Anchor Healthcare is revolutionizing patient education with AI-powered video libraries tailored for medical specialties. Their goal? Bridge the communication gap between doctors and patients, at scale.

With HatchWorks AI, they built and launched an AI-powered platform that enables physicians to deliver consistent, understandable medical content using custom digital avatars and explainer videos.

The Challenge

Consistent, medically accurate education without the repeat conversations

What Anchor set out to do

  1. 01

    Automate high-quality patient education

    Automate the delivery of high-quality, medically accurate patient education.

  2. 02

    Offload repetitive conversations

    Enable physicians to offload the repetitive conversations that consume clinic time.

  3. 03

    Reduce liability, improve outcomes

    Reduce liability risks and improve healthcare outcomes through clearer, more consistent explanations.

The Process

Stitch best-of-breed AI into one engine, then launch

  1. 01

    Agile co-creation

    HatchWorks AI joined forces with Anchor's hybrid team in an agile sprint process, collaborating daily, selecting best-of-breed AI point solutions, and validating each integration through proof of concept.

  2. 02

    Stitching the ecosystem

    Rather than building custom AI from scratch, the team stitched together avatar platforms, video generators, and cloud services into a seamless engine, integrated via APIs and hosted on GCP.

  3. 03

    Building the engine

    HatchWorks AI delivered the production-ready AI engine that generates custom physician avatars to explain hundreds of conditions and their associated aftercare.

  4. 04

    Launching the MVP

    The platform launched with 282 explainer videos, initially focused on pain medicine. It is now used daily by Dr. Robert Moghim, a practicing anesthesiologist and co-founder of Anchor Healthcare, and is capable of generating hundreds, if not thousands, of videos across multiple specialties.

The Outcome

Live with real patients and doctors

Anchor's MVP is live, used by real patients and doctors. Once configured, the system can generate 80 custom videos per hour per physician, and an entire custom video library for a new doctor can be created in under a day.

Physicians save time, patients get clarity, and care outcomes improve.
