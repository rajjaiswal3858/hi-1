Overview

Airspace telemetry, turned into plain-English answers

DronePort Network is piloting Meerir, an AI platform that turns low-altitude airspace telemetry into plain-English answers and simple visuals.

The MVP connects a proprietary, real-time ADS-B feed to a large language model so airport managers, city planners, and the public can ask questions and get decision-ready metrics in seconds.

The Challenge

Below 4,000 feet, the sky is busy and the data is hard to read

Below roughly 4,000 feet, the sky is busy: traditional aircraft, drones, and emergency operations share the same airspace. The data about it is fragmented, hard to access, and difficult to interpret without deep aviation expertise, which slows planning, grant work, and public-safety decisions.

Stakeholders needed an accessible way to see what's flying overhead and quantify activity around local airports.

Fragmented

Telemetry scattered across sources, with no single place to ask a question.

Hard to access

Getting at the data meant tooling and time most stakeholders don't have.

Hard to interpret

Raw feeds need aviation expertise before they mean anything to a planner or a council.

What We Built

A live feed, a language model, and the guardrails between them

Meerir ingests and preprocesses the real-time ADS-B feed, applies geospatial filtering and time-series aggregation, and hands the result to a large language model that answers in plain language. Guardrails on coordinates and uniqueness rules increase reliability on time-series telemetry.

The Outcome

Decision-ready insights in seconds

A production MVP now returns accurate, plain-language answers from live telemetry in seconds. Early stakeholder feedback confirms commercial potential for communities and public-safety teams, and the platform gives airport operations faster situational awareness, planning support, and baseline analytics.

A functioning conversational AI interface powered by large language models.

Robust ADS-B ingestion and preprocessing for reliable analytics.

Initial visuals focused on traffic density and airport operations.

A scalable foundation for dashboards, new feeds, alerting, and expansion.
