Overview

One platform for every system, built with AI at every stage

ALTA AI is redefining cost management by unifying SaaS, Cloud, Hardware, and Data Centers onto a single platform. To truly optimize systems, they needed dozens of seamless integrations to provide customers with comprehensive system control.

HatchWorks AI developed an MVP that combined traditional software development with a Generative-Driven Development™ (GenDD) approach. GenDD leverages advanced AI models to automate coding tasks, integration logic, and ongoing maintenance workflows.

The Challenge

Dozens of integrations, and every one of them keeps changing

Enterprise customers require platforms that easily manage diverse systems and rapidly adapt to external API and infrastructure changes. Two major challenges stood in the way.

  1. 01

    Extensive integration requirements

    The platform had to seamlessly support a broad range of SaaS, cloud, and hardware systems.

  2. 02

    Rapid delivery and maintenance

    Quick development cycles and automated maintenance were essential to handle frequent external API updates.

The Process

Prioritize with pilot customers, then let AI carry the integration work

  1. 01

    Collaborative discovery and prioritization

    HatchWorks AI worked closely with ALTA AI and its pilot customers to identify and prioritize the most critical system integrations, mapping the integration landscape before writing code.

  2. 02

    Accelerated development with GenDD

    Using GenDD, which leverages AI at every stage, the team integrated Cursor, a tool that supports full-stack development, to significantly speed up the coding and integration process. This reduced an estimated 20 business days' worth of effort into less than 5 business days.

  3. 03

    Ongoing maintenance, automated

    Automated AI analysis minimizes human intervention for API changes, and an iterative review checks accuracy and functionality with each integration update.

Lesson: scope selection

Well-defined processes were selected to best leverage generative AI.

Lesson: a defined MVP

Clear criteria for integration completion were set to match customer needs.

The Outcome

Integration time cut from 20 to under 5 business days

Embracing a GenDD approach quickly transformed complex integration challenges into a scalable, cost-efficient solution. By integrating automated maintenance capabilities and continuously iterating on customer feedback, the project met its aggressive timeline and enhanced the platform's overall value proposition for enterprise customers.

For organizations facing the challenges of managing complex, ever-changing systems, the GenDD approach offers a breakthrough that shortens development time, cuts ongoing support costs, and delivers an outstanding customer experience.
