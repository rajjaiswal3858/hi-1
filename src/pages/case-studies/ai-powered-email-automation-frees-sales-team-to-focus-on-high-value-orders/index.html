Overview

Tens of thousands of emails a month, seven people to answer them

A Los Angeles based wholesale manufacturer and supplier of promotional products needed a way to keep up with tens of thousands of customer emails every month, without burning out its seven-person inside sales team.

Working with HatchWorks AI, they implemented an AI-powered email automation system that reads every incoming message, identifies which ones truly need responses, pulls real-time order data from NetSuite, and drafts accurate replies directly in Microsoft Outlook.

The result: nearly half of response-worthy emails are now handled automatically, giving the sales team more time to focus on complex, high-value customer requests.

The Challenge

Repetitive questions were crowding out the work that drives revenue

The company's inside sales team is the front line for customer communication. Each month, they receive tens of thousands of emails from distributors and resellers, ranging from simple "What's my order status?" questions to complex, custom requests.

Over time, the team's bandwidth was increasingly consumed by repetitive order-status questions, routine policy clarifications (shipping, returns, timelines), and low-value notifications that didn't actually require a response.

The impacts were clear

  1. 01

    Limited capacity for complex requests

    High-value, nuanced customer inquiries competed with repetitive emails for attention.

  2. 02

    Rising operational strain

    Manually triaging and replying to thousands of emails was exhausting for the seven-person team.

  3. 03

    No baselines for improvement

    The company lacked clear visibility into which emails required human responses, what could be automated, and how accurate automation would be.

The company needed an automation solution that could

  • Automatically handle high-volume, repeatable questions

  • Maintain brand tone and accuracy

  • Integrate with Outlook and NetSuite

  • Give leadership real metrics on coverage, accuracy, and adoption

The Process

Four components, one email engine

HatchWorks AI partnered with the company to design and implement an AI-powered email automation engine built around four key components: classification, response generation, quality verification, and user feedback.

  1. 01

    AI-powered classification engine

    The first step was determining which emails should be automated at all. HatchWorks AI built an email classification engine on Google Cloud Platform (GCP) leveraging OpenAI to read each incoming message, classified emails into in-scope categories suitable for automation (order status, shipping timelines, policy clarifications), and identified out-of-scope messages that required human attention (custom orders, escalations, edge cases). This layer ensured AI only touched the types of messages where automation could be both safe and valuable.

  2. 02

    AI response engine in n8n

    Once in-scope emails were identified, a multi-agent workflow in n8n connected to NetSuite to fetch order details such as status, shipment dates, and relevant policy information. An OpenAI node generated draft responses based on the structured data and the original email content. Responses were formatted to match the company's brand voice and support expectations, then surfaced directly in users' Microsoft Outlook inbox.

  3. 03

    LLM-as-judge quality verification

    To maintain trust, a dedicated "LLM as a judge" step was added within the n8n workflow. This verification layer evaluated each AI-generated response against quality criteria such as relevance, completeness, and clarity. Only responses meeting the quality threshold were delivered to users, so automation drove efficiency without sacrificing user experience.

  4. 04

    User feedback module and adoption analytics

    Internal users could quickly signal whether an AI-generated response was helpful or needed improvement. Usage and adoption metrics captured how often AI was triggered, how frequently responses were accepted versus edited, and where additional training or policy refinement was needed. This closed loop lets the company and HatchWorks AI continuously fine-tune prompts, classification logic, and response patterns based on real-world performance.

The Outcome

Email went from a bottleneck to a strategic asset

The AI-powered automation system gave the company measurable gains in both efficiency and insight: 47% automation coverage for emails that genuinely required responses, clear visibility that nearly 50% of incoming emails did not require any response at all, and up to 70% send-ready responses for routine inquiries, with a built-in review path for edge cases.

The inside sales team now spends less time on repetitive status checks and more time on complex, relationship-driven work that improves customer satisfaction and drives revenue. The automation framework is reusable and can be extended to new email categories, policies, or product lines over time.

Rather than guessing how much of the inbox could be automated, the company now operates with data-backed insights and a proven system that is already handling a substantial portion of support load.
