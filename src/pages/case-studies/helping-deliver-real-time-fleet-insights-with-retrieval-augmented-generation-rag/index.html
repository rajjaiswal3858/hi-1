Overview

Fleet data, answered in natural language

Cox2M, a leading provider of commercial IoT solutions headquartered in Atlanta, GA, partnered with HatchWorks AI to develop the Kayo AI Assistant for their fleet management customers.

The assistant provides real-time trip analysis and fleet metrics through HatchWorks AI's RAG Accelerator, allowing fleet managers to use natural language for easy and accurate access to complex data across a wide range of use cases.

The Challenge

Every new report was a development cycle

Cox2M wanted to offer its customers real-time fleet insights and metrics within their existing IoT solution. As their customer base grew, the development of real-time data reports became increasingly complex and resource-intensive.

Aggregating and presenting vast amounts of fleet data, from mileage and hard braking to trip start and end times, in a user-friendly format was the challenge Cox2M sought to address with the Kayo AI Assistant.

What customers needed

  1. 01

    Real-time metrics without long development cycles

    Fleet metrics on demand, instead of a new report build for every question.

  2. 02

    Simplified access to the data that matters

    Trip start and end times, hard braking events, and total mileage for tax purposes.

  3. 03

    A natural-language interface that gets it right

    A user-friendly interface capable of delivering accurate, real-time responses using natural language processing.

The Process

From a workshop to a working assistant

  1. 01

    Gen AI Innovation Workshop

    The journey started with HatchWorks AI's Gen AI Innovation Workshop, guiding the Cox2M team through identifying key opportunity areas where generative AI could drive value.

  2. 02

    A shared vision, a secure approach

    The workshop helped establish a clear vision for the Kayo AI Assistant and aligned the team on using AI in a secure fashion.

  3. 03

    Design and build with RAG

    HatchWorks AI designed and developed the Kayo AI Assistant, which leverages Retrieval Augmented Generation to connect a large language model to Cox2M's fleet data securely, so users ask questions of their data through natural language and get real-time, accurate responses.

  4. 04

    Infrastructure, architecture, and UI/UX

    The teams collaborated on infrastructure, architecture, and UI/UX, producing a scalable, flexible, and cost-effective solution across cloud platforms like Google Cloud and Vertex AI.

The Outcome

Accurate answers, faster delivery, and a new sales tool

The Kayo AI Assistant delivered exceptional response accuracy on user queries. It significantly reduced development time and gave Cox2M a scalable platform to expand functionality as new use cases arise.

Customers can now easily access real-time fleet insights, resulting in time and resource savings. The cost-effective and flexible infrastructure ensures scalability across cloud environments, valuable for attracting new customers while retaining existing ones. The assistant stands out as a sales tool for Cox2M.
