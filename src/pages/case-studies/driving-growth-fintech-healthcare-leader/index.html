Overview

A consulting firm's spin-off, built into a fintech healthcare leader

A US-based healthcare consulting firm launched a SaaS analytics spin-off to help healthcare providers benchmark Medicare revenues and improve financial decision-making. With HatchWorks' expertise, the startup evolved into a fintech healthcare leader, scaling rapidly while transforming financial analytics for healthcare providers.

The Challenge

A new revenue stream, without the in-house engineering to build it

A healthcare consulting firm sought to create a new revenue stream by launching a SaaS analytics platform. The goal was to provide healthcare providers with automated financial benchmarking and analytics to improve their Medicare revenue strategies.

However, as a professional services firm, they lacked the in-house technical expertise to develop the platform. To turn their concept into a market-ready product, they needed a partner with full lifecycle software development expertise.

Real-time financial intelligence, scaled from startup to market leader

The custom analytics platform streamlined the collection, processing, and reporting of key patient data, providing healthcare facilities, insurers, and corporations with powerful insights.

By leveraging cloud-based automation and intuitive user workflows, HatchWorks helped the startup rapidly scale its offering, delivering real-time financial intelligence to its target market.
