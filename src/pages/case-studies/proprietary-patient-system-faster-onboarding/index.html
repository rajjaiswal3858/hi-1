Overview

A system built for specialty pharmacy, not adapted to it

Clearway Health, a specialty pharmacy services company based in Boston, partnered with HatchWorks AI to build Charter, a proprietary patient management system (PMS) engineered to meet the unique demands of specialty pharmacy workflows. Charter replaces a simpler system that limited customization and innovation.

By leveraging AWS infrastructure, Clearway Health now possesses a flexible, scalable solution that offers pharmacy and operations teams a more efficient and insightful approach to patient management and engagement.

The Challenge

Teams were adapting their workflows to fit the tool

Clearway Health previously relied on a commercially available software tool that constrained customization and forced their teams to adapt workflows to fit the tool. This hindered innovation, created data segmentation, and required time-consuming manual updates.

The Process

Align the teams, choose the cloud, then iterate

HatchWorks AI aligned Clearway Health's clinical and operations teams around a vision for a custom, cloud-native PMS. Through workshops and collaborative architecture sessions, AWS was chosen for its long-term scalability, cost efficiency, and seamless integration with existing enterprise applications. Development then proceeded iteratively.

  1. 01

    Architecture and infrastructure

    Designed for modularity, real-time data synchronization, and secure integration with other systems.

  2. 02

    Data integration and workflow design

    Workflows to support patient engagement, prior authorization tracking, financial assistance, and care plan updates.

  3. 03

    UI/UX design

    User research led to an intuitive interface that reduces manual entry, streamlines task prioritization, and centralizes information.
