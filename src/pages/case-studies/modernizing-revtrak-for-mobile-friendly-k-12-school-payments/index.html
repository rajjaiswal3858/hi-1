Overview

A responsive, scalable RevTrak without touching what schools depend on

Vanco Payments is a financial technology provider specializing in payment processing and donation tools for community organizations, including K-12 schools. RevTrak is part of Vanco's education offering, helping schools manage a wide range of school-related payments in one place.

To better support today's families and reduce friction on mobile, Vanco partnered with HatchWorks AI to modernize RevTrak into a responsive, scalable web experience, without disrupting the backend services and integrations schools already rely on.

The Challenge

Legacy AngularJS, and parents who expect to pay from their phone

RevTrak was running on legacy AngularJS, which limited mobile usability and made it harder to evolve the product quickly.

With parents increasingly expecting a simple, mobile-friendly way to pay for meals, field trips, athletics, spirit wear, and more, Vanco needed a modern frontend foundation that could scale across payment categories and devices.

Legacy framework

AngularJS limited mobile usability and slowed product evolution.

Mobile expectations

Families want to pay for meals, trips, and athletics from a phone.

Nothing can break

Backend services and integrations schools rely on had to keep working.

The Process

Re-engineer the frontend, preserve the ecosystem

HatchWorks partnered with Vanco to re-engineer RevTrak into a modern, component-based Angular application designed for responsiveness, maintainability, and long-term growth. Key workstreams included:

  1. 01

    AngularJS to Angular migration

    Migrated RevTrak pages to modern Angular using a component-based approach to improve maintainability and consistency across the product.

  2. 02

    Mobile-ready, parent-friendly UX

    Built responsive interfaces and refreshed UI patterns to reduce friction across core payment and account-management flows.

  3. 03

    Reduced complexity in key journeys

    Simplified how users find fees and complete payments: navigation depth cut from 5 levels to a maximum of 3.

  4. 04

    Backend and ecosystem integration

    Connected the new UI to existing Vanco APIs, authentication services, data stores, and third-party integrations, with zero continuity disruption.

  5. 05

    Platform foundation for ongoing delivery

    Improved frontend-backend separation to support maintainability and long-term development velocity.

The Outcome

Less friction for families, no disruption for schools

The modernized RevTrak experience helps schools and families complete payments with less friction, especially on mobile, while keeping operational workflows consistent behind the scenes.

Frictionless mobile payments. Families pay for meals, field trips, and athletics from any device: fewer steps, cleaner experience.

Uninterrupted school operations. Backend integrations fully preserved, with no disruption to the administrative workflows schools already rely on.

Fewer avoidable support issues. Simplified navigation and clearer flows reduce payment errors and cut unnecessary support requests.

Methodology

How GenDD supported delivery

Alongside standard engineering practices (code review, testing, and QA), the team used GenDD-enabled AI tools to accelerate delivery and improve quality across both frontend and backend work.

  1. 01

    Backend audit and refactor planning

    Auditing and understanding the existing backend to identify required refactors and new endpoints for modernization, giving the team a clear map of what to preserve, what to evolve, and where to extend.

  2. 02

    Contract-first API design

    Generating and refining OpenAPI (YAML) specifications to clearly define API contracts, including routes, parameters, and response formats. This enabled parallel frontend and backend work and reduced integration churn.

  3. 03

    Figma to production UI

    Accelerating frontend development by translating Figma designs and mockups into production-ready Angular components, shortening the path from design to shipped, mobile-friendly interfaces.

  4. 04

    Knowledge gap acceleration

    Assisting with knowledge gaps, including integrating internal payment and card-processing libraries, using documentation review and AI-assisted code analysis to close ramp-up time on unfamiliar systems.

GenDD-enabled delivery across frontend and backend, accelerating modernization without sacrificing stability for the schools and families relying on RevTrak.
