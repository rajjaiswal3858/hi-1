Overview

At a crossroads with digital-first competitors

One of North America's leading on-demand staffing firms found themselves at a crossroads, with digital-first competitors primed to capture their market share. They needed a digital transformation to evolve a business that dispatches over 300K temporary workers a year.

The transformation had to be cost effective, without sacrificing quality or disrupting current customers' experience, and it had to run on real-time collaboration and Agile delivery. The goal: transition from aging technology to a more agile and scalable digital platform with a dynamic pricing model, drawing on HatchWorks' expertise in software development, cloud migration, machine learning, and digital transformation.

The Challenge

Declining revenue, inflexible technology

The firm faced declining revenues due to the rise of the gig economy, and its existing technology infrastructure lacked the flexibility to respond. The challenge was twofold.

  1. 01

    Rapidly scale up with the right technical expertise

    The transformation needed more engineering capacity, and the right skills, faster than in-house hiring could deliver.

  2. 02

    Do it cost-effectively without compromising on quality

    Keep the current customer experience intact while the platform changed underneath it.

The Process

Proof of concept first, then pods that scale with demand

  1. 01

    Assess and prove

    HatchWorks assessed the existing technology stack, understood the requirements, and developed a proof of concept, providing design and product expertise from the start.

  2. 02

    Start with two pods

    A dedicated team of software developers from Latin America was integrated, starting with 2 pods and then scaling up to meet demand, supporting both new product development and support of the legacy solution.

  3. 03

    Embed machine learning

    The team additionally embedded machine learning into the client's solution.

  4. 04

    Collaborate in real time, transition without disruption

    Aligned US and Latin America time zones enabled an Agile approach with real-time collaboration, regular updates, and a seamless transition from the legacy system.

The Outcome

A modern platform, and a path for growth

The engagement delivered a modern digital platform that addressed the firm's immediate challenges and set them on a path for future growth, in support of the 300K people they put to work every year. The new system allows the firm to adapt to industry changes swiftly.

Cost savings came in at approximately half of what they would have incurred with onshore alternatives. What began as a transformation project became a long-term, continuously beneficial partnership rather than a one-and-done engagement.
