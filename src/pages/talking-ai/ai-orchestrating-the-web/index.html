Orchestrating the Web: The Untapped Potential of AI

  • Andy Smith
  • April 16, 2024
  • Updated: March 10, 2025

Welcome to an exciting episode of the Built Right podcast where we are talking to Alexander De Ridder, who is an entrepreneur, technologist, and visionary, focused on leveraging AI to transform businesses. Alexander is also the Co-Founder and CTO of SmythOS.

In this episode, Alexander gives us an overview of his fascinating career journey, which has led to where he is today, segueing into an in-depth look at the concept of AI orchestration and the future of AI agents. Alexander explains where he sees AI going in the future and challenges the conventional view of the web as merely a collection of information, presenting it instead as an evolving organism.

The conversation also covers the importance of orchestrating the web, the concept of SmythOS, which aims to create networks of collaborative orchestrating agents, his predictions around the transition to AI agents, and how employees can adapt to this rapidly changing landscape. Plus, much more.
