Why Most Companies Aren’t Getting ROI From AI

  • Andy Smith
  • November 17, 2026
  • Updated: November 12, 2026

In January, Brandon Powell sat down with five of HatchWorks AI’s best engineers and learned they had stopped writing code. Agents did the work. So what did the engineers do while the agents ran? After an awkward silence, one of them told him: “We play video games.”

In this episode of Talking AI, Matt Paige sits down with Brandon Powell, CEO of HatchWorks AI and author of the new book The AI ROI Engine. Brandon was the first guest on the Built Right podcast back in 2023. He returns to share what HatchWorks AI learned pivoting the whole company to AI, and what hundreds of client implementations reveal about why most AI spend never turns into a return.

His core argument: better tools don’t create ROI on their own. If you don’t change the structure of your teams and your organization, the time AI saves simply disappears.

In this episode, you’ll hear about:

  • Why HatchWorks AI went all in on AI, and why the first year was so hard
  • The five-by-one meeting that changed how Brandon thinks about team structure
  • Why engineering is the canary in the coal mine for every other function
  • The five places companies spend on AI, and the one that matters most
  • Why AI is an enabler of strategy, not a strategy
  • How GenROI brings input from the whole organization into AI prioritization
  • The six failure modes that sink AI initiatives, from the wrong use case to pilot purgatory
  • What makes a company AI native, and why those companies never hire the middle
  • Small pods, forward deployed engineers, and one-person teams
  • The three levers of AI ROI: profit, risk reduction, and decision velocity
  • The AI ROI formula: potential value times adoption reality, minus true cost
  • Brandon’s predictions on agents, org design, and open source models
