How AI Drives the Multiple

Three ways we drive enterprise value.

Every engagement ladders to the number that matters at exit.

Drive cost out

Grow revenue without growing headcount.

  • Custom automation and AI agents take high-cost, high-volume work off the payroll.
  • Underwriting, customer service, document processing, demand forecasting, quality inspection.
  • The result is a structural lift to EBITDA margin that buyers underwrite directly.

Transform how the business runs

Move a traditional operator into the tech-enabled category.

  • We modernize the data and pipelines underneath the business.
  • We re-engineer the core workflows that move the work, and the products customers touch.
  • AI becomes the way work gets done. That category shift is a different conversation with a buyer, and a different multiple.

Build proprietary IP they own

Owned AI becomes a defensible asset at exit.

  • Off-the-shelf AI gives efficiency any competitor can buy tomorrow.

  • Companies with proprietary AI are commanding 15–20% valuation premiums.
