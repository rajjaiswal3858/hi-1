Join us for a special HatchWorks AI Lab in partnership with Baird & Warner, a leader in the real estate industry providing residential and commercial brokerage, mortgage, and title services with over 160 years of experience.


Why Attend?

  • Organizations spend significant time and effort gathering and reconciling data from different systems—a process known as Master Data Management (MDM).
  • There are a number of MDM tools (COTS, vendor-managed, OSS) but even with these, success is not guaranteed.
  • Many MDM tasks still require manual effort.
  • Gen AI can help automate these time-consuming tasks, such as data deduplication.


In This Lab

  • We’ll use Gen AI to automate one of the most manual processes in MDM: data deduplication.
  • We’ll then use RAG to ask the data questions through natural language to uncover unique insights.


Who is this for?

This lab is ideal for CIOs, CTOs, CDOs, and their teams who are interested in leveraging Generative AI for MDM.
