Explore the Databricks Sandbox with Julian Arango

Welcome to the HatchWorks AI Lab on Databricks! In this session, Julian Arango walks you through the essentials of the Databricks Sandbox account—a powerful environment designed to help Hatchers learn, experiment, and innovate.

What You’ll Discover:

  • Getting Started: Learn how to access the Databricks Sandbox using your corporate email and navigate the initial login, including two-factor authentication.
  • Platform Walkthrough: Follow along as Julian demonstrates key features such as the Partner Labs, learning resources, and step-by-step guides to set up your workspace.
  • Interactive Learning: Discover how to run and manage notebooks, explore data catalogs powered by Unity Catalog, and tap into the innovative gene AI capabilities for SQL analysis and visualization.
  • Practical Tips: Get insights on downloading and migrating your notebooks, managing external data, and understanding best practices for creating secure and efficient workspaces.


Why Watch This Video?

Whether you’re new to Databricks or looking to deepen your understanding of the platform, this video is a must-watch. Julian’s clear instructions and hands-on demonstration will guide you through the environment so you can make the most of the tools at your disposal.
