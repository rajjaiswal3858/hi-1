FAQ

Common questions

How is this different from staff augmentation?

Staff augmentation adds capacity. A HatchWorks AI Forward Deployed Engineer adds AI execution capability. FDEs are embedded builders focused on identifying high-value use cases, shipping production AI systems, and helping your team build momentum.

Do HatchWorks AI FDEs only work with one model or platform?

No. The offering is model-neutral. HatchWorks AI works across the enterprise AI stack and brings certified partner expertise across Anthropic, Google Cloud, and Databricks.

When should we use an FDE instead of an Agentic AI Pod?

Use an FDE when you need a senior AI builder embedded with your team. Use an Agentic AI Pod when the scope requires a compact cross-functional team to own a larger product, platform, or workflow automation initiative.

Is this right for us?

FDEs are a strong fit if you have AI pilots that have not reached production, valuable data spread across systems, or product and engineering teams that need more AI-native delivery expertise.
